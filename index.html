01 · About

ECE by degree.
Builder by nature.

I chose Electronics and Communication Engineering because I wanted to understand technology from the ground up — from the transistor to the interface. But somewhere in my first year, writing my first lines of JavaScript, I realized software was the fastest way to turn an idea into something real. So I started learning both, at the same time, on purpose.

Artificial Intelligence fascinates me because it sits exactly where my two worlds meet — it needs the rigor of electronics thinking and the creativity of software design. I don't see myself as "an ECE student who also codes." I see myself as an engineer who refuses to pick a lane.

My long-term goal is simple to say and hard to do: build products, at the intersection of hardware and AI, that positively impact millions of people — starting with HealSphear AI, in pet healthcare.

04 · Flagship Project

HealSphear AI

AI-powered pet healthcare — helping owners understand, monitor, and act on their pet's health.

In Active Development

An AI health companion for pets.

Live Demo GitHub ↗

HealSphear AI is an AI-powered platform built to help pet owners monitor and care for their animals with the same seriousness we bring to human healthcare. It combines an AI health assistant, symptom and vital tracking, and a hospital-finder into one experience — with the long-term goal of becoming a complete intelligent healthcare ecosystem for pets.

AI Health AssistantConversational triage that helps owners understand symptoms.
Health MonitoringTracks vitals and habits over time to flag anomalies early.
Hospital FinderLocates nearby vet hospitals when urgent care is needed.
Health TrackingA running record of visits, vaccinations, and conditions.

Technologies

ReactNode.jsPython AI / ML APIsMongoDBREST API

Challenges

Designing an AI assistant that's genuinely useful without overstepping into medical diagnosis, and building a data model flexible enough for dozens of species and breeds.

Future Scope

Expanding into a full intelligent healthcare ecosystem for pets — wearable integration, vet-verified AI triage, and a connected network of clinics.

Overview

Started as a personal project to solve a real gap: pet owners rarely have fast, trustworthy guidance when something feels wrong with their animal.
